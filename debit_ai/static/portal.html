Open the live demo See the task circuit ↓

No account. Runs locally with one command: ./demo.sh

The task circuit

  1. Receive & splitEach file becomes lines with literal payer motives and amounts.
  2. Route tasksEvery line gets tasks per sector: Billing, Medical Audit, Agreements.
  3. Answer with backingSectors reply with a document or a precise, checkable reference.
  4. Débitos verifiesThe backing is accepted, or the task is returned stating what is missing.
  5. Resolve per lineIndividual resolution — or accepted debit with origin analysis. No mass close.
  6. Rebill & trackLinked rebilling invoice, partial recognitions, balance in dispute, shared audits by email.

What you can verify

The model does
Classify motives, propose routing, draft referrals — every finding carries a code-checked citation.
The model never does
Clinical judgment, merit or money conclusions, auto-accept, or sending messages anywhere.
Data
Fully fictitious fixtures; synthetic provider by default; no real documents, patients, or identifiers.
Decisions
Every resolution, acceptance and recovery is recorded by a named human, with date and rationale.
